SRAD: Smooth Refinement with Adaptive Delineation for Fattening Building Edges in Satellite DSMs

Sun Yat-Sen University · Wuhan University · Hubei Luojia Laboratory

Brief Introduction

Satellite photogrammetry provides broad-coverage Digital Surface Models (DSMs), but building boundaries often suffer from edge-fattening caused by dense matching smoothness constraints. Fixed-buffer DSM post-refinement can miss local fattening widths or include nearby ground objects, while height-similarity refinement may create abrupt and unnatural transitions.

SRAD addresses these issues with two connected designs. Height Potential Contour Tracing (HPCT) adaptively delineates fattening-edge buffers from building footprints and DSM heights. Gradient Consistent Refinement (GCR) then optimizes heights inside these buffers using plane labels and gradient consistency, preserving smooth topographic transitions while correcting building edges.

Framework

SRAD separates where to refine from how to refine. HPCT estimates adaptive interior and exterior buffers around each building footprint from the height potential field, and GCR updates only these selected edge regions before merging the refined heights back into the initial DSM.
